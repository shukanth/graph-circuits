Key observations
  1. Stage 2 is the dominant signal — WU, WE, L1H0 WQ, and L1H1 WQ all show anomalous
     behavior precisely at the 188k–192k window. This is the "circuit snap."
  2. Stage 1 (114k–125k) shows no sharp changes — consistent with a gradual ramp rather
     than a phase transition in weight space.
  3. L1H0 WK declines monotonically throughout training (4.365 → 3.364) regardless of
     either transition — continuous weight decay compression.
  4. L1H0 WQ and L1H1 WQ diverge at Stage 2 — one rises, one falls. Layer 1 queries
     are reorganizing at the second transition.
  5. WU is non-monotone: dips during Stage 1, then climbs strongly through Stage 2
     and beyond (19.6 → 21.6). Unembedding growing post-grokking suggests the model
     is sharpening its output class separation after finding the generalizing circuit.
